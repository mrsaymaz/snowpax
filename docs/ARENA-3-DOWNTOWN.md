# Arena 3 · Downtown

Downtown is an evening loop through Beyoğlu: from Galata Tower up İstiklal Avenue to Taksim Square, down the hill through Cihangir, and on to Tophane by the water. The light follows the evening: golden at Galata, festive lights on İstiklal, blue hour at Taksim, café lamps in Cihangir, then night on the water at Tophane. Downtown opens once the Golden Horn is won.

## The outpost: the nostalgic tram

The red nostalgic tram of İstiklal runs round a ring of track under its overhead wire. An old street clock and the DOWNTOWN sign stand in the middle. The five places sit round the edge as the tram's stops, in task order. The tram pulls up at the place being played. Places light up when their task is done, and lit places lob snowballs in the fights.

## The places, their tasks and their views

| Part | Place | Task | The landmark and its view on the sled ride |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Galata Tower | Sorting | The round stone tower with its gallery and pointed cap, a bakery and a dessert shop at its foot, the queues at the door (golden late afternoon; across the water the old city's domes). |
| 1 | İstiklal Avenue | Builder | The red tram on its rails under strings of lights; the Flower Passage's grand front; the old school gate, shown without crest or name (dusk; Galata Tower at the far end). |
| 2 | Taksim Square | Listening | The opera house's glass front with its red sphere of a hall, the tall hotel tower (no name), the park's snowy trees, a red and white city bus (blue hour; the Şişli towers far off). |
| 2 | Cihangir | Matching | Pastel houses with bay windows on the steep slope, café tables under the big plane tree, an antique shop, cats everywhere (evening; below, the Bosphorus, the Maiden's Tower, the bridge lit up). |
| 2 | Tophane | Answer | The old clock tower and the marble fountain on the promenade, the art museum, a cruise ship at the quay (night; across the water the Maiden's Tower, the bridge and the old city's domes). |

Places of worship stay scenery only. Nusretiye Mosque appears in the Tophane view behind the houses, never in a task scene or on the outpost. The Republic Monument is left out. Shops, cafés, the hotel and the cruise port carry no names.

## The task scenes

| Place | Task | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| Galata Tower | Sorting | The queues at the tower's door, one line per category behind brass posts and red ropes, a sign over each. Pick a word ticket, then its line: a visitor in a bobble hat walks up and joins the queue. A wrong line: the visitor stops, shakes, and walks across to the right one. |
| İstiklal Avenue | Builder | The motor car waits at the end of the rails. Tap the words in order and each tram car is coupled behind it. A finished tram rides off up the avenue with a ding, and the next is built on the same rails. Spelling lays ice letters the same way. |
| Taksim Square | Listening | City buses wait at the stop, each showing a word on its lit route sign. Hear the word and wave down the right bus: its headlights flash, it honks and pulls forward. A wrong bus blinks its hazard lights. |
| Cihangir | Matching | The neighbourhood's cats sit on doorsteps, each with a word on its collar tag. Tap a cat, then its partner: the two pad over, curl up together, and a heart floats up. Their tails swish while they wait. |
| Tophane | Answer | Round clock faces stand along the promenade railing, one answer on each. Snowball the right one: it glows, the clock tower's hands spin round and its bell chimes. A wrong one gets a splat of snow. |

## Music

| Piece | Sound |
|---|---|
| The sled ride | an upbeat swing with sleigh bells, a walking bass and a clarinet's tune |
| The arrival | the tram's bell twice, then a jaunty jazz fanfare |
| Galata Tower | a sweet bakery waltz on a music box |
| İstiklal Avenue | the tram's bell, then a swing tune on a muted trumpet over a walking bass |
| Taksim Square | a city bus's horn, then a soaring aria line over warm strings |
| Cihangir | a lazy café bossa on a soft piano, and a cat's meow at the end |
| Tophane | the clock tower's chimes, lapping water and a slow clarinet tune |

## The field

The avenue: newspaper kiosks, benches, snowbanks left by the ploughs, a roadworks barricade, bushes by the park and ice on the tram rails. The rival team brings sledders racing down the steep streets, and the Final Showdown is played at Tophane.
