# English tasks at the outposts (M2)

Each outpost is its landmark, and each landmark has one task family. The cards are themed to
match.

| Outpost | Card | Family | Arena 1 sub-tasks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kızılay Square | Kızılay Fountain (blue tiles) | Matching | A1 English ↔ Turkish, A2 word ↔ picture |
| Boulevard Park | The Treehouse (wood) | Answer | B8 clue → word, B9 sentence ending |
| Archery Ground | Archers' Lodge (marble, red and gold) | Sorting | C15 sort into groups |
| Sururi Park | The Snow Castle (pastel snow blocks) | Builder | D19 build the sentence, D20 spell the word |
| Waterfront Park | The Admiral's Bell (navy and brass) | Listening | E21 listen and pick |

## A visit

1. **Reach.** Walk, tap the map, or ride a sled.
2. **Seize.**
   - Step into the circle and the card slides in from the right. The fight stays visible on the
     left, because the camera shifts.
   - The student stays put and the two teammates guard them.
   - Arena 1 has 2 rounds of 3 items.
   - Every right answer fires a volley from the outpost and fills the bar.
   - A rival snowball that gets through only chills the bar for a moment. It never closes the
     card or hurries an answer.
   - The student can't be frozen while answering.
   - "Step out" leaves; the answers given so far stay.
   - The rivals can't take the circle while the student is answering.
   - An outpost the rivals hold can be won back the same way.
3. **Defend.**
   - When the bar is full the outpost is yours, and the rivals counterattack for about 14 s.
   - Then one short round (3 items) follows. Each right answer refills everyone's snowballs.
4. **Held.** The outpost becomes a respawn point, its lanterns turn your colour, and the coach
   reports the first-try score so far.

## Answers

- **Right first time:** the answer goes green and the next step follows. Sentences and words are
  read aloud, and the Turkish meaning shows when the bank has one.
- **Wrong:** a kind line from Mr. Saymaz, and the right answer glows or is shown. The task
  carries on; nobody gets stuck.
- **Scoring:** every item (each pair, card, sentence or word) counts once toward the arena's
  first-try accuracy (the 80% rule). The pause menu shows the score so far.
- **Missed words:** they are remembered per unit and come back more often, for example on the
  snowflakes.
- **Challenge pace:** each step has a timer, for example 14 s for a choice or 27 s for three
  pairs. It pauses while the voice is speaking. Time up counts as a miss and shows the answer.
  The Relaxed pace has no timer.
- **Listening:** this uses the device's English voice, with a 🔊 button to hear it again. With
  no voice, the card shows the word to read instead.

## Hot cocoa and snowflake words

- **Hot cocoa.** There are five cups on the map. A cup asks one question first; only the student
  can drink.
  - A right answer gives full warmth, full snowballs and some super power.
  - A wrong answer: the cup comes back in 40 s.
- **Snowflakes.** They are golden, with their English word on a tag. Picking one up shows the
  word with its Turkish meaning and reads it aloud. The all-time total is saved.
