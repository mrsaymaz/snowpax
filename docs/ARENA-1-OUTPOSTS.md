# Arena 1: Kasımpaşa, its outpost and places

Arena 1 has no real map and no real streets (Mr. Saymaz's decision after the second play-test),
and since the fifth talk it has **one outpost, Kasımpaşa** (each later arena, such as İstiklal
Street, will have one too). The places Mr. Saymaz chose are all part of it, each with its own
English task and scene (`tools/build-world.mjs`, see `docs/STAGES.md`):

| Place | Family | Landmark | Where on the field |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boulevard Park | B · Answer | the treehouse | west side, behind the fence |
| Archery Ground | C · Sorting | an archers' lodge | north-west, behind the rival camp |
| Kızılay Square | A · Matching | the fountain (no Red Crescent emblem) | the outpost's centre |
| Sururi Park | D · Builder | the snow castle | north-east, behind the rival camp |
| The Pier | E · Listening | the admiral's statue | east side, behind the fence |

Part 1 of the English is Boulevard Park and Archery Ground; Part 2 is Kızılay Square, Sururi Park
and the Pier.

## Rules kept

- Our School is only ever "Our School". Other schools never appear.
- No shop names, brands or football club crests; stalls are generic (simit, çay, salep, chestnut).
- No places of worship or cemeteries are in the game world, so none can ever be fought near.
- No homes of students are marked: there is no real map at all.
