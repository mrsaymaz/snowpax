# Arena 1: Home Turf, outposts

Arena 1 has no real map and no real streets (Mr. Saymaz's decision after the second play-test).
Each stage is a purpose-built field (`tools/build-world.mjs`, see `docs/STAGES.md`); only the look
of the neighbourhood is kept round it. The five outposts keep the names Mr. Saymaz chose, as
themes for their fields:

| # | Outpost | Family | Landmark | The field's theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Boulevard Park | B · Answer | the treehouse | park hedges, plane trees |
| 2 | Archery Ground | C · Sorting | an archers' lodge | a wooden practice ground |
| 3 | Kızılay Square | A · Matching | the fountain | a town square (no Red Crescent emblem) |
| 4 | Sururi Park | D · Builder | the snow castle | snow ramparts |
| 5 | Waterfront Park | E · Listening | the admiral's bell | the quay by the frozen Golden Horn, a ferry and a pier |
| — | Our School | Team base + Schoolyard Showdown | Our School and its bell | the playground |

## Rules kept

- Our School is only ever "Our School". Other schools never appear.
- No shop names, brands or football club crests; stalls are generic (simit, çay, salep, chestnut,
  roasted corn, fish sandwiches).
- No places of worship or cemeteries are in the game world, so none can ever be fought near.
- No homes of students are marked: there is no real map at all.
