# Arena 1: Kasımpaşa, its outpost and places

Arena 1 has no real map and no real streets (Mr. Saymaz's decision after the second play-test),
and since the fifth talk it has **one outpost, Kasımpaşa** (each later arena, such as İstiklal
Street, will have one too). The places Mr. Saymaz chose are all part of it, each with its own
English task and scene (`tools/build-world.mjs`, see `docs/STAGES.md`):

| Place | Family | Landmark (a miniature on the carousel, a scene on the sled ride) |
|---|---|---|
| Boulevard Park | B · Answer | the treehouse |
| Archery Ground | C · Sorting | an archers' lodge |
| Kızılay Square | A · Matching | the fountain (no Red Crescent emblem) |
| Sururi Park | D · Builder | the snow castle |
| The Pier | E · Listening | the admiral's statue |

The outpost itself is a carousel: a ship's mast with an anchor and the KASIMPAŞA sign in the
middle (for the old naval shipyard), the five landmarks riding round it.

Part 1 of the English is Boulevard Park and Archery Ground; Part 2 is Kızılay Square, Sururi Park
and the Pier.

## Rules kept

- Our School is only ever "Our School". Other schools never appear.
- No shop names, brands or football club crests; stalls are generic (simit, çay, salep, chestnut).
- No places of worship or cemeteries are in the game world, so none can ever be fought near.
- No homes of students are marked: there is no real map at all.

## What each view looks out at

The sled ride's views face Kasımpaşa's real surroundings, a little larger than life. Domes and minarets appear only as distant scenery.

| Place | Background |
|---|---|
| Boulevard Park | down the valley: the old city's domes across the water; Galata Tower on its hill |
| Archery Ground | from the hilltop: over the Golden Horn to the old city; Galata Tower to the left |
| Kızılay Square | up the hill to Galata Tower; the tall towers of Şişli far off |
| Sururi Park | the towers of Şişli in the low sun; a cypress hill; Galata Tower |
| The Pier | across the Golden Horn: the old city's ridge of domes; the bridges to the left |

## Street life in the views

Each view has its own street life, placed clear of the camera's path. Every landmark has flower beds and a street cat beside it, so a phone sees them too.

| Place | Around the landmark |
|---|---|
| Boulevard Park | lights strung down the avenue of plane trees, the chestnut and salep stalls steaming, café tables, benches, lamps, flower beds, snowmen, a sled, pigeons, flags |
| Archery Ground | arrow racks by the straw targets, pennants and tall flags along the back, a tea garden by the salep stall, spectator benches, cypresses, pigeons |
| Kızılay Square | flocks of pigeons, tea gardens round the square, lights strung across the back, steaming stalls, benches, lamps, flower beds, flags |
| Sururi Park | a rocking see-saw, sleds left in the snow, snowmen, pennants over the castle, string lights, benches, lamps, pigeons |
| The Pier | quay lamps and benches, the railing with fishing rods and buckets, bollards, café tables, rowing boats frozen in the ice, plane trees, pigeons, flags |

The string lights twinkle, pennants and flags flutter, steam rises from the stalls and tea glasses, pigeons peck and hop, the see-saw rocks and the cats swish their tails.
